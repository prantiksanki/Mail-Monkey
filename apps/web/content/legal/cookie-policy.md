This Cookie Policy explains how MailMonkey ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and application. By using MailMonkey, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide functionality, and collect information about how visitors use the site. Cookies may be set by us ("first-party cookies") or by third-party services we use ("third-party cookies").

---

## 2. Types of cookies we use

### Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for MailMonkey to function properly. They enable core functionality such as authentication, security, and session management. The service cannot operate without these cookies.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Session cookie | Maintains your authenticated session | Session |
| CSRF token | Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |
| OAuth state | Secures OAuth authentication flows | Session |

### Functional cookies

These cookies allow MailMonkey to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Language preference | Remembers your preferred language | 1 year |
| Theme preference | Remembers your display preferences | 1 year |

### Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with MailMonkey by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve the service.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Analytics identifier | Distinguishes unique users anonymously | 2 years |
| Session analytics | Tracks session-level interactions | 30 minutes |

## 4. How to manage cookies

### Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

- View cookies stored on your device and delete them individually
- Block third-party cookies
- Block cookies from specific sites
- Block all cookies from being set
- Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent MailMonkey from functioning properly.
