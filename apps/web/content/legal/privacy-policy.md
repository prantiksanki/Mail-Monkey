## 2. Data we collect about you

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

- **Account data:** Name, email address, and account credentials
- **Google account data:** Name, email address, and profile picture obtained via Google OAuth
- **Gmail data:** Email messages, email metadata (sender, recipient, subject line, date, thread ID), labels, and mailbox folder structure
- **Microsoft Outlook data:** Email messages, metadata, folders, and mailbox structure
- **Meeting and CRM integration data:** If you connect Zoom, Zoho Meeting or HubSpot, your account identifier and OAuth tokens for that service, and (for HubSpot) contact, company and deal records for the people you email
- **Usage data:** Features used, rules and automations created, and interaction patterns within MailMonkey
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, time zone, and log data

We may also create aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized data from personal data we collect. This anonymized data cannot be used to identify you and may be used to analyze, improve, and promote MailMonkey.

## 6. Google user data

**Specific data accessed:** When you connect your Google account, MailMonkey accesses the following through the Gmail API:

- Gmail messages (read, modify, archive, or delete — as directed by you or your automation rules)
- Email metadata: sender, recipient, subject line, date, and thread identifiers
- Gmail labels and folder structure
- Google profile information: name and email address

**How we use Google user data:** Google user data is used solely to provide and improve the MailMonkey features you request. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating MailMonkey.

**Limited Use Policy:** MailMonkey' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. This applies to all data obtained through Google APIs — including raw data, as well as any data aggregated, anonymized, or derived from it. Specifically, we do not:

- Use Google user data to serve advertisements
- Use Google user data to profile users for purposes unrelated to the app
- Sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties
- Use Google user data for any purpose beyond providing and improving MailMonkey
- Use aggregated, anonymized, or derived data from Google APIs for any purpose inconsistent with these restrictions

**Human access to Google user data:** We do not allow any person to read your Gmail messages or email content, except in the following limited circumstances:

- You have given us your explicit prior consent to view a specific message or thread
- It is necessary to investigate a security issue or abuse
- It is required to comply with applicable law
- The data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations in accordance with applicable privacy law

**Revoking Google access:** You can revoke MailMonkey' access to your Google account at any time by visiting [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and removing MailMonkey. You may also contact us to disconnect your account directly.

## 7. AI processing of your data

MailMonkey uses AI and machine learning to power inbox automation features such as rule generation, email categorization, and smart suggestions.

**Data shared with AI models:** To deliver these features, the following data may be processed by AI models:

- Email subject lines
- Email body text
- Sender and recipient information
- Labels and folder names

**No AI training on your data:** We do not allow our AI service providers to train their models using your personal data or email content. Your data is used only to generate results for you within the MailMonkey service.

**Zero data retention:** We operate under a zero data retention policy with our AI providers, meaning your data is not stored on AI provider servers after a response is generated.

**Your consent:** Before sharing your email data with AI models, we obtain your explicit consent within the MailMonkey application.

## 9. Zoom, Zoho Meeting and HubSpot user data

You can optionally connect Zoom, Zoho Meeting and HubSpot to MailMonkey. These connections are separate from your mailboxes, are off until you connect them, and are used only for the purposes below.

- **Zoom:** When you connect Zoom we request permission to create meetings (`meeting:write:meeting`) and read your basic Zoom profile (`user:read:user`). We store your Zoom user ID and the OAuth access and refresh tokens (encrypted at rest). We use them solely to create a Zoom meeting link when you ask MailMonkey to add one to an email or calendar invite. We do not access your Zoom recordings, chat, contacts, or existing meetings.
- **Zoho Meeting:** When you connect Zoho Meeting we request permission to create meetings (`ZohoMeeting.meeting.CREATE`) and read your basic Zoho profile (`AaaServer.profile.READ`). We store the resulting OAuth tokens (encrypted at rest) and use them solely to create meeting links when you ask.
- **HubSpot:** When you connect HubSpot we request read access to contacts, companies and deals, and permission to create contacts. We use this to show CRM context for the people you email and to create a contact only when you ask us to. We do not modify or delete existing CRM records.

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than the service providers described in Section 10.

**Disconnecting:** You can disconnect any of these integrations at any time from Settings → Integrations, which deletes the stored tokens. If you remove MailMonkey from your Zoom account (through the Zoom App Marketplace), Zoom notifies us and we delete the stored Zoom tokens for your account automatically. Meeting links already created remain in your emails and calendar events and in your Zoom, Zoho or HubSpot account, which are governed by those providers' own privacy policies.

## 12. Data security

We take the security of your data — especially sensitive Gmail and Outlook data — seriously. Our measures include:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data between your browser, our servers, and third-party APIs is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security)
- **Encryption at rest:** Email data and personal information stored on our servers is encrypted at rest
- **Access controls:** Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel on a strict need-to-know basis
- **OAuth 2.0:** We use official OAuth 2.0 protocols — we never store your Google or Microsoft passwords
- **Data breach procedures:** We have procedures in place to detect, investigate, and respond to personal data breaches. We will notify you and any applicable regulator of a breach where legally required to do so

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at `support@mailmonkey.app`.

## 13. Data retention and deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet our legal obligations.

**Account deletion:** You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at `support@mailmonkey.app`. We will delete your personal data within **30 days** of a verified request, except where retention is required by applicable law.

**Mailbox disconnection:** When you disconnect a linked Google or Outlook account, we stop accessing that mailbox and delete cached email data associated with it within **30 days**.

- **Object** to processing where we rely on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at `support@mailmonkey.app`. We will respond to all legitimate requests within **one month**. If your request is complex, we may extend this by a further two months and will notify you accordingly. There is no fee to exercise these rights.
