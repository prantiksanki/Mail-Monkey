## 2. Data we collect about you

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

- **Account data:** Name, email address, and account credentials
- **Google account data:** Name, email address, and profile picture obtained via Google OAuth
- **Gmail data:** Email messages, email metadata (sender, recipient, subject line, date, thread ID), labels, and mailbox folder structure
- **Microsoft Outlook data:** Email messages, metadata, folders, and mailbox structure
- **Financial data:** Billing information and payment details (processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers)
- **Usage data:** Features used, rules and automations created, and interaction patterns within Zer0Email

We may also create aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized data from personal data we collect. This anonymized data cannot be used to identify you and may be used to analyze, improve, and promote Zer0Email.

## 6. Google user data

**Specific data accessed:** When you connect your Google account, Zer0Email accesses the following through the Gmail API:

- Gmail messages (read, modify, archive, or delete — as directed by you or your automation rules)
- Email metadata: sender, recipient, subject line, date, and thread identifiers
- Gmail labels and folder structure
- Google profile information: name and email address

**How we use Google user data:** Google user data is used solely to provide and improve the Zer0Email features you request. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating Zer0Email.

**Limited Use Policy:** Zer0Email's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. This applies to all data obtained through Google APIs — including raw data, as well as any data aggregated, anonymized, or derived from it. Specifically, we do not:

- Use Google user data to serve advertisements
- Use Google user data to profile users for purposes unrelated to the app
- Sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties
- Use Google user data for any purpose beyond providing and improving Zer0Email
- Use aggregated, anonymized, or derived data from Google APIs for any purpose inconsistent with these restrictions

**Human access to Google user data:** We do not allow any person to read your Gmail messages or email content, except in the following limited circumstances:

- You have given us your explicit prior consent to view a specific message or thread
- It is necessary to investigate a security issue or abuse
- It is required to comply with applicable law
- The data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations in accordance with applicable privacy law

**Revoking Google access:** You can revoke Zer0Email's access to your Google account at any time by visiting [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and removing Zer0Email. You may also contact us to disconnect your account directly.

## 7. AI processing of your data

Zer0Email uses AI and machine learning to power inbox automation features such as rule generation, email categorization, and smart suggestions.

**Data shared with AI models:** To deliver these features, the following data may be processed by AI models:

- Email subject lines
- Email body text
- Sender and recipient information
- Labels and folder names

**No AI training on your data:** We do not allow our AI service providers to train their models using your personal data or email content. Your data is used only to generate results for you within the Zer0Email service.

**Zero data retention:** We operate under a zero data retention policy with our AI providers, meaning your data is not stored on AI provider servers after a response is generated.

**Your consent:** Before sharing your email data with AI models, we obtain your explicit consent within the Zer0Email application.

## 11. Data security

We take the security of your data — especially sensitive Gmail and Outlook data — seriously. Our measures include:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data between your browser, our servers, and third-party APIs is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security)
- **Encryption at rest:** Email data and personal information stored on our servers is encrypted at rest
- **Access controls:** Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel on a strict need-to-know basis
- **OAuth 2.0:** We use official OAuth 2.0 protocols — we never store your Google or Microsoft passwords
- **Data breach procedures:** We have procedures in place to detect, investigate, and respond to personal data breaches. We will notify you and any applicable regulator of a breach where legally required to do so

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at `founder@zer0email.com`.

## 12. Data retention and deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet our legal obligations.

**Account deletion:** You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at `founder@zer0email.com`. We will delete your personal data within **30 days** of a verified request, except where retention is required by applicable law.

**Mailbox disconnection:** When you disconnect a linked Google or Outlook account, we stop accessing that mailbox and delete cached email data associated with it within **30 days**.
